Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

This policy explains what AFK Live collects, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. AFK Live is run from Bengaluru, India, and follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

Your account: your name, email address and a protected (hashed) password, or your Google account's name and email if you sign in with Google. Your time zone, currency and alert choices.

What you stream: the videos, music and images you upload, your streams' settings, overlays and schedules, and the destinations you add. Stream keys are stored encrypted and are never shown in full again.

Billing: your plan, payments and invoices, including the billing name, address and GSTIN you give us. Card and UPI details are handled by our payment provider, PhonePe; we never see or store them.

Running the service: technical records such as IP addresses, browser type, sign-in times, stream health and error logs, used to keep streams up and accounts secure.

Devices for alerts: if you turn on alerts on a device, the address your browser gives us for sending them.

2. How we use it

To run your streams and send them where you choose, to bill you and issue GST invoices, to send the alerts and emails you've asked for, to keep accounts secure (for example two-step sign-in and rate limits), and to fix problems. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

3. Who helps us

DigitalOcean hosts our servers and your files (in India). Cloudflare runs our domain's DNS. PhonePe processes payments. Zoho ZeptoMail sends our emails. Google handles Google sign-in if you use it. The platforms you stream to (YouTube, Facebook and others) receive your stream under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to sign you in, remember you if you ask us to, and protect forms from forgery. No advertising or tracking cookies.

5. How long we keep it

As long as your account is open. When you delete your account, it's switched off straight away and deleted with its files after 7 days (you can change your mind until then). Invoices are kept as Indian tax law requires; personal details are removed from them where the law allows.

6. Your choices

You can download a copy of your data and delete your account in Settings. You can turn email and device alerts off, per kind of alert. For anything else, including correcting your data or a complaint, write to support@afklive.in; we reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, write to our grievance officer.

7. Children

AFK Live isn't meant for anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian managing the account.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll email account holders before it takes effect.
